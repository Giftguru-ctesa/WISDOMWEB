About & academics

A Montessori foundation. A community built on wisdom.

Since 2003, Wisdom Academy Schools has nurtured children in Abuja into intellectually independent, spiritually sound and disciplined young people.

Established 2003 Abuja, FCT Montessori based

Our story

From six pupils to a community of learners.

Wisdom Academy opened its gates to its first pupils in September 2003, with four members of staff and six pupils. By the end of that first term, the six had grown to thirty.

Today, more than two decades later, 485 pupils learn here beside 35 experienced staff, guided by the vision and tenacity of our Proprietress, Mrs. Cecilia Nwiyi — affectionately called Mommy Wisdom.

Our serene campus in Abuja gives children the peace and space to learn, explore and grow into confident, disciplined young people.

Purpose

A vision and mission we live daily.

Everything we do is measured against one question: are we building a total child?

Our vision

We have a vision of building a total child who is intellectually independent and spiritually sound in Abuja and its environs.

01

Our mission

To build the foundation of a child centred on academic excellence, discipline, leadership and extra-curricular education for the making of a total child.

We challenge ourselves to keep finding better ways to raise the standard of education in Nigeria.

02

This promise is protected by our zero tolerance to examination malpractice, indiscipline, child abuse and lazy teachers.

Learning at Wisdom

A Montessori foundation. A lifetime of independent thinking.

Every pupil is known, challenged and supported. Our classrooms combine the structure of a high-standard curriculum with the freedom of Montessori discovery.

Foundation stage

Early years

The early years are a time of wonder. Our Montessori setting gives young learners the freedom to explore, practise and grow in independence.

  • Practical life and sensorial learning
  • Early language and numeracy
  • Confidence, order and independence
Core stage

Primary years

A rigorous yet child-centred programme that stretches thinking, builds strong literacy and numeracy, and lays the groundwork for lifelong learning.

  • Literacy, numeracy and science
  • Critical thinking and creativity
  • Leadership, character and service
All pupils

Enrichment

Beyond the timetable, every pupil is invited to discover a talent, serve a cause and grow in faith, culture and community.

  • Arts, music and sport
  • Environment and cultural studies
  • Spiritual and social development

Montessori materials, qualified teachers and small classes help every child understand deeply, not just memorise. See life beyond the classroom
