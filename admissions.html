Admissions

Give your child a place to grow.

We are delighted that you are considering Wisdom Academy Schools. Our admissions process is warm, personal and designed to help you make the right choice for your family.

Admissions open 2026/2027 session Abuja, FCT

How it works

Four simple steps to joining us.

From your first question to your child’s first day, we will walk with you at every stage.

01

Enquire

Send us a message or call into the school office. Tell us a little about your child and what you are looking for, and we will explain the next steps.

02

Visit & meet

Book a campus visit to see our learning spaces, meet our teachers and experience the calm, purposeful atmosphere of the school.

03

Apply

Complete the application form and provide the required documents. We will confirm receipt and keep you informed throughout.

04

Assessment & offer

Your child will take part in a friendly, age-appropriate assessment and conversation. Successful families receive an offer and a joining pack.

Before you apply

What you will need.

To help us process your child’s application smoothly, please have the following documents ready.

  • A completed application form
  • Your child’s birth certificate or age declaration
  • Most recent school report or progress record, where applicable
  • Two recent passport photographs
  • Transfer certificate for pupils changing schools

Fees, places & timing
