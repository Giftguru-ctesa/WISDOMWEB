History
Wisdom Academy School opened its gates to its first set of pupils in September, 2003. She started with staff strength of 4 and 6 numbers of pupils. The 6 numbers of pupils hit 30 pupils at the end of the same term. Today, after 12 years of weathering the storm, with a nurtured organizational asset of well-experienced teachers coupled with the vigor of the visionary Proprietress, the school is delighted to her performance and has enrolled 485 pupils with about 200 pupils present. Her staff strength also considerably increased to 35 staffs. Wisdom Academy is confidently positioned in a location of absolute serenity, conducive to groom your ward.
