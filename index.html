Welcome to Wisdom Academy

A foundation that lasts a lifetime.

We are keen on a child’s early start and their future aspirations. Because the foundation of a child has a long-lasting effect on their entire life, we build strong roots that position every pupil on the right side of life.

Here, pupils grow to excel academically, stand spiritually sound and become disciplined citizens of the nation and the world at large. It is a promise we have kept since 2003.

  • A serene, secure campus in Abuja, FCT
  • Professionally qualified and dedicated teachers
  • A proven Montessori approach to early learning
